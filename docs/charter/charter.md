## 2. Mission Statement / Project Goal

The creation of a web application that marks and manages optically scanned exams.

The application will mark, analyze, and return optically marked exams. This solves the current issue of marking these tests, since current software and hardware are costly and hard to schedule during exam season.

---

## 3. Team Goals

### Academic Goals

- To create a 90%+ project that we would be proud to put on our resumes.

### Collaboration Goals

- Complete milestones ahead of time.
- Schedule in-person meetings twice per week to keep everyone in constant communication.
- Plan ahead as a team.

### Deliverables and Milestones

- Initial architecture and repository planning
- Testing and validation
- MVP implementation
- Final deployment and presentation

---

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

### Technical and Organizational Roles

- Everyone does their own documentation.
- Scrum master.

### Initial Responsibilities

- Every team member must be in contact with the team at least twice a week.
- Team members should respond to online messages promptly, within approximately 2 hours.

### Role Rotation / Revisions

As development continues, the team will shift towards feature specialization. Each member will be responsible for their feature throughout development.

---

## 5. Working Norms and Expectations

### Communication Tools

- Discord

### Meeting Frequency and Format

- In-person meetings after classes on Tuesdays and Fridays.
- Online meetings scheduled as needed.

### Code Repository Conventions

- One branch per feature.
- Brief descriptive commit messages.
- Further explanation should be included in pull requests.

### Code Review / Pull Requests

- 2 reviewers per pull request.

### Task Management

- GitHub Issues via Kanban board.

### Accommodating Learning and Working Styles

People can do tasks according to their strengths and can get help from others during meetings or online if necessary.

---

## 6. Conflict Resolution Plan

### Addressing Disagreements

Smaller conflicts will be discussed as a whole team during meetings. If there is a split vote, the team will spin a wheel or do a coin flip.

### Handling Unresponsive or Underperforming Members

If a team member is unresponsive after 5 days without a valid excuse, the team member might be expelled from the group.

---

## 7. Decision-Making Process

Decisions will be made by general consensus, followed by a vote if needed.

During split votes, if a decision cannot be made, the team will leave it up to random choice, such as doing a coin flip.

---

## 8. Accountability and Commitment

### Attendance, Contributions, and Deadlines

- Every team member is required to show up to class.
- Every team member is required to attend the scheduled meetings after class.
- If someone will be absent, they must communicate that with the team at least 1 week ahead of time.

### Missed Deadlines or Check-ins

If someone repeatedly misses deadlines or check-ins, the team will discuss whether escalation to the TAs or professor is necessary.

---

## 9. Review and Revision

The charter will be revised as a team after the Minimum Viable Product presentation.
